What these numbers mean

Depth estimate starts with the whole-account mark, replaces Polymarket midpoint values with recorded bid-depth estimates after trading fees, and subtracts separately funded gas tokens. It includes cash, Aave deposits and other managed assets. It is an indicative valuation, not settled cash or guaranteed liquidation proceeds; quote times differ and routing or withdrawal costs are omitted.

Midpoint value subtracts funded gas from the whole-account mark. Thin books can make this look better than the available exit price. Return is value divided by contributed trading capital, minus one. The recent history has no external capital flows; this is not a time-weighted return calculation.

SPY shows only recorded, validated benchmark observations, dated to the market session they value. It assumes the same contribution dates, ideal fractional shares and reinvested distributions, without commissions, tax or execution costs. Missing observations stay missing; points connected on the chart are not daily prices.

Historical snapshots are backfilled from the journal, weekly reports and dated repository dashboards. Missing fields remain blank. A tilde (~) marks an approximate scheduled-check time; date-only observations are labeled separately. VM/API costs are unreconciled and excluded from every pilot value shown here.
