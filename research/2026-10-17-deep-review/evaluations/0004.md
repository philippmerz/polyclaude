## Evidence and competing interpretations

[NASA's FAQ](https://science.nasa.gov/uap/faqs/), updated October8 and read in full on October17, says NASA has no credible evidence of extraterrestrial life and cannot infer an extraterrestrial explanation from UAP observations. This is agency evidence, not proof every eligible official will remain silent. NASA links to the Department of War disclosure programme, so the programme was checked rather than dismissed as social-media speculation.

The [official PURSUE release page](https://www.war.gov/UFO/release/05/?agency=Office+of+the+Director+of+National+Intelligence) describes rolling declassification and labels its archive as cases for which government cannot determine the phenomena's nature. The page's indexed official text was available; direct requests returned403 and this access limit is preserved. Five published tranches through August7 establish an actual political disclosure channel. They do not themselves assert an extraterrestrial origin. No multi-gigabyte archive was assumed reviewed.

The [official May27 presidential transcript](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202600370/html/DCPD-202600370.htm), paragraphs concerning the files, was checked after finding a claim that the President had already admitted aliens. In context he discusses releasing space-related information and public interest, without definitively identifying life or technology as extraterrestrial. That is my literal interpretation, not a binding oracle ruling. Likewise the [White House aliens page](https://www.whitehouse.gov/aliens/) explicitly concerns immigration: its alien language is not a qualifying life claim.

[NASA's September10,2025 Mars release](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-says-mars-rover-discovered-potential-biosignature-last-year/) describes suggestive chemical/mineral evidence, remaining nonbiological explanations and further verification. Its cautious wording fails the definitive-statement test. It is a real scientific pathway and was not mislabeled a new2026 confirmation. The current FAQ continuing to state no credible life evidence reduces the chance this older release has already fulfilled the condition. This search cannot certify the absence of every eligible historical remark.
