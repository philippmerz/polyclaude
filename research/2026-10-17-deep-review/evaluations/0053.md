The dissenters preferred holding because inflation and economic strength did not justify that step. A7–2 vote for a recent25bp move is not a7/9 probability of a fresh50bp move.

## Primary probability construction

I allocate about20% to any additional October hike after September's recent move, and7.5% conditional on a hike to a move above25bp after rounding, yielding1.5%. The first weight balances inflation/FX upside against the short interval and weak domestic-demand evidence. The second reflects a central-bank preference for gradual moves and the absence of a captured explicit double-size warning, while retaining an import-price/yen or communication shock. Both weights are judgmental, not fitted frequencies, and the0.1–8% range admits a much more aggressive interpretation of Tokyo/wages or a fresh policy signal. No current OIS distribution or policy-maker commitment was verified; no market probability supplies these weights.

The key distinction is between **direction and size**. Evidence making a25bp hike more likely does not raise this bucket equally. A shock can also cause an intermeeting move, resetting the anchor so the eventual meeting change is smaller. The next national CPI and official BOJ speeches matter more than assuming all current inflation observations persist unchanged for two weeks. The horizon is short, but the rare policy response is not a measured binomial tail and does not qualify for a K5 exception.
