## Primary tail construction

Central 0.3% is an explicit judgment decomposition: approximately 3% probability of a severe enough monetary/financial regime break before year end, times 10% conditional probability it produces at least 12 counted cuts by the deadline. Ordinary gradual policy contributes negligibly to the large conventional hurdle; the peculiar many-tiny-cuts route is possible but has no observed current institutional support. These are scenario weights, not a historical frequency estimator. The 0.01–2% range permits a much smaller crisis incidence or a much more forceful response. For a tiny probability, uncertainty in either factor dominates expected return.

The separate October/December scenario model covers ordinary actions and does not pretend to include all extraordinary paths here. September's already-announced hike does not disqualify subsequent cuts. A crisis could produce only 150–250 bp by year end or a major cut in January, paying NO despite the macro thesis being direction ally correct. This is a timing-and-magnitude tail, not general crash insurance.
