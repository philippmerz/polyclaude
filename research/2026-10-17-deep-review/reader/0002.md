## Primary model and explicit uncertainty

The executable model and parameters are saved in `data/deep_review_20261017/spotify_primary_sensitivity.json`; seed 20261017, 60,000 draws for each cutoff scenario. This is a transparent judgment model, not an empirically calibrated forecast. I impute the **average across all seven missing weeks together** from a triangular 5.5/7.5/10 million distribution, informed by the visible late 2025/January 29/February 26 anchors. Drawing one average prevents seven independent draws falsely making missing data seem precise. It is an assumption that can be wrong, not reconstructed evidence.

Future average weekly pace is anchored to the latest observed week and given persistent log normal uncertainty: Man I Need median multiplier 1.02/log-sigma .20, Earrings .98/.15, Babydoll 1/.25 and Mr.Brightside 1/.15. A 3 million additive methodology/cumulative noise term applies to each track; Earrings/Babydoll get 0–2 million for the missing opening date. Choosin' Texas is assumed to retain #1 given its large lead. Other-cohort or aggregation outcomes reserve 10% of modeled success; that reserve is subjective and may be insufficient.

Hypothetical cutoff horizons 30/44/77 days receive 70/20/10% judgment weights. They represent roughly mid-November, end-November and calendar-year alternatives, not confirmed 2026 policy. Conditional probabilities that Man I Need beats the next three named contenders are about 50.6/41.9/31.7% for those horizons. The mixture is 46.9%, discounted to 42.3% for omitted-competitor/aggregation risk, rounded to 42%. This is the whole construction, with no market-probability input.

The key sensitivity is **missing early-week listening**, not Monte Carlo sampling error. Low missing-week averages or extended cutoff favour Earrings; stronger early listening and a short window favour Man I Need. The 5–80% range admits those alternatives plus unknown official aggregation. It is not a 95% statistical confidence interval. Verifying historical January/February US counts and official cutoff could move the estimate by tens of percentage points. Until then I require at least a 20 pp case-specific probability reserve, larger than the ordinary 10 pp floor. This follows from an identified input gap rather than a blanket demand for certainty.
