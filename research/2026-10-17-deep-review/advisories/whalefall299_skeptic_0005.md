# Whalefall 299 — skeptical review

The contract is narrowly defined: **YES only if** the finalized Oct. 16–18 domestic three-day gross is below $8 million; exactly $8 million resolves NO. Thursday previews normally belong in the weekend total. If finality is ambiguous, the rule says to wait for both The Numbers and Box Office Mojo; Oct. 23 is the alternate-source deadline, not an automatic resolution date. See the [frozen rule and current source facts](../../../data/deep_review_20261017/work/0299_facts_2355.json).

The strongest challenge to NO is that the present $8.0m is explicitly estimated, not final, and sits exactly at the cutoff. A small downward true-up is enough for YES. The Numbers’ daily cells show $3.1m/$2.8m/$2.1m, also estimates and rounded; they do not reveal the unrounded true total. The separate $0.9m preview must not be added again. Mojo also displays $8m, but its generic estimated-capable row is not independent proof of finality. Thus apparent cross-source agreement may simply repeat the same estimate.

The corrected Sunday-to-final sample is five films from **one** Oct. 9–11 weekend, not five independent weekends: four revisions were upward and one downward. The two opening-film cases split: Other Mommy rose 1.1%, while The Social Reckoning fell 8.8%; three holdovers rose 1.9–4.8%. That one downward opening revision is large enough to take an $8m estimate below threshold. It does not estimate this film’s error distribution, and the mixed studio/The Numbers estimates, film mix, and shared weekend make even a 4/5 count weak evidence. The prior six-pair draft mixed Friday/Saturday estimates and an unsupported title; it is superseded by the [root correction](../../../data/deep_review_20261017/work/0299_calibration_root_correction_0005.json).

The model’s 42.9% YES is therefore a judgmental revision mixture, not a calibrated posterior. Its 5% exact-$8m atom and 5% broad source branch are subjective; the 15–75% range is more honest than its point estimate. A YES probability above 76.3% (NO below the roughly 23.7% fee-adjusted NO break-even) is possible if the Sunday estimate is systematically high for this specific opening or the final report cuts even modestly. The sample does not establish that; equally, four upward revisions do not justify treating $8m as a final lock.

Before treating NO as underpriced, require a fresh exact-title table read after The Numbers’ next update and confirmation of its estimate/final marker, with Mojo’s corresponding dated row. Keep the $8m boundary, preview inclusion, and fallback timing literal. No independent probability or entry recommendation is offered.
