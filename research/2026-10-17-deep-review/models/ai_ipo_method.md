## Evidence and what it establishes

[Anthropic's June 1 notice](https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec)
and [OpenAI's June 8 notice](https://openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/)
establish confidential draft submissions, not achieved listings. Current bounded
SEC/exchange directories and issuer news checks found no direct company stock
listing. ANTW/OAIW matches are ecosystem ETFs, not issuer equity. These checks
do not prove worldwide absence or eliminate a missed announcement.

[SEC's current FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-corporation-finance/voluntary-submission-draft-registration-statements-faqs)
describes a 15-day public-registration/draft-release interval before a roadshow,
or effectiveness without one. It is staff guidance for the described process,
not a universal prohibition against every listing route. Roughly 75 days remain;
the procedure does not itself prevent a November/December completion.

The [original September 12 interview](https://fortune.com/2026/09/12/sam-altman-interview-ai-doomsday-safety-models-control-ipo-2027/)
records Altman's intention to wait until 2027. It is a strong timing signal,
not a binding corporate undertaking. The source packet links the original video;
we have not independently transcribed its audio. OpenAI's June option notice is
older than this timing statement and cannot erase it.

[Reuters, October 15/16](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/investors-temper-anthropic-ipo-valuation-expectations-over-revenue-growth-slowdown-sources-say-ce785dd3d18ef225)
reports a recent Anthropic investor meeting, a planned November sale, and some
investors favoring about $1.5T rather than the earlier $2T expectation. Anonymous
sources and no company confirmation limit its force. This is substantial
current evidence of preparation, not proof of a completed transaction. The
[September prospectus report](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/exclusiveanthropics-ipo-prospectus-shows-sweeping-ai-vision-surging-costs-4921382)
provides earlier preparation context. Operating losses, accounting charges and
multiyear infrastructure commitments must not be conflated. Root read rendered
articles; direct capture attempts returned 403 and remain documented gaps.

[Anthropic's May financing](https://www.anthropic.com/news/series-h) reports
$65B raised at $965B post-money and revenue run rate above $47B then. That
private valuation is dated, uses different security terms, and is not a floor
or a prediction of the official first-day public closing capitalization.

[Nasdaq's account of SpaceX's debut](https://www.nasdaq.com/newsroom/spacex-makes-history-raising-85-billion-through-nasdaq-listing)
reports a June 12 session near $160.95 and $2.1T capitalization. The SEC-hosted
offering capitalization gives 7,380,196,910 Class A and 5,695,668,265 Class B
base post-offering shares. Their product with $160.95 is about **$2.10456T**.
The later June 15 closing included the full underwriter option; its larger
share count is a separate timing sensitivity. Opening capitalization near
$1.77T, money raised and today's stock price do not replace this first-close
comparison. The official first-day exchange capitalization is the preferred
resolution evidence; the share calculation is an audit, not a universal rule
about diluted shares. No larger completed 2026 IPO was substantiated in the
bounded pass, which is not proof none exists worldwide.

## Explicit primary probability construction

The three regimes in `ai_ipo_model.py` jointly model whether an Anthropic IPO
finishes by year-end and the conditional first-day price. Their subjective
weights are 45% orderly offering with concessions, 40% weak demand, and 15%
market/safety/governance interruption. Completion chances within them are
95%, 65% and 15%. Thus P(issuer IPO) is **71%**. These are judgments anchored
by preparation and remaining time, not historical frequency estimates.

For first-day capitalization, median offer values are $1.75T/$1.50T/$1.25T,
median first-close/offer multiples 1.12/1.05/1.00, and log dispersions
.23/.25/.35. The offered values and first-day moves are hypotheses, not prices
announced by Anthropic. The model explicitly admits pricing near the older $2T
target, a strong opening and a disappointing sale. Completion and pricing
share regimes rather than being treated as independent. A further 94% conditional
survival against other eligible IPOs accounts approximately for a surprise
larger rival, including a timing reversal by OpenAI. This yields about **18%**
for Anthropic having the largest eligible first-day close. Sparse rival coverage
and the joint dependence are meaningful uncertainties.

For case199, an explicit 1% nonstandard-listing branch on remaining paths adds
about .29pp to the issuer-sale probability. Both cases round to **71%**, but
their complete payoff functions are not declared fungible. A direct listing,
merger or an offering/trading time split requires a new literal interpretation.
For OpenAI, a 6% timing reversal times 75% timely completion, plus .5% other
eligibility/missed-event mass, gives **5%**. No claim says reversal is impossible.

The saved 81-case sensitivity grid varies common completion, valuation, opening
move and rival assumptions. Wider primary ranges are 25–97% Anthropic IPO/listing,
.1–35% OpenAI IPO, and 1–75% Anthropic largest IPO. These are scenarios, not
confidence intervals or certified entry floors. Ten-point ordinary stress is
a decision reserve; no measured-tail exception is claimed.

## Route and management

Each exact condition has separate ten-share both-side book walks, nonlinear
fees, depth, quote age, minimum and exit estimates. The older two Anthropic
timing asks exceed their stressed probabilities; current money decisions must
refresh them. Case158 NO appears worth a small passive follow-up at the dated
64c vicinity: central NO82%, stressed72%, rather than a certain bet against an
IPO. The IPO could occur and this NO still win if its first-day cap loses the
comparison. A strong first-day move can defeat the thesis.

Before any order, primary must read independent champion and skeptic notes,
finish current news coverage, and recheck current exact rules, scalar UMA status,
fees, books, legal access, balances, full pending commitments and correlated
portfolio caps. Never rest a stale bid through a known filing, pricing or
trading window. Public filings can precede ordinary news; insider/private
demand and overnight first-day gaps remain. Dates are not yet confirmed.
Normal early-January settlement fits the project horizon, but disputes or a
halted first session can delay it. The 76-day Aave comparison is a variable-rate
carry sensitivity; funding/deployment is not assumed free. No order or canonical
portfolio prior is created by this model.
