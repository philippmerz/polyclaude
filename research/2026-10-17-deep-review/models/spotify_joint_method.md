## Shared probability construction and its limitations

`spotify_joint_inputs.json` preserves the leading50 observed row-window
tracks plus all eleven named children,54 distinct exact IDs. This is an
incomplete cohort selected from219 successfully parsed current/legacy track
histories, not an exhaustive annual universe. The actual rank is computed
within each draw rather than fixing Choosin' Texas as #1, though its424.459m
observed lead strongly favors that result in the central scenario.

The seed202610172 model runs20,000 draws for each hypothetical November15,
November30 and December31 cutoff, weighted70/20/10%. These dates are explicit
primary assumptions, not announced2026 policy. Future weekly pace uses one
persistent lognormal multiplier per track plus a common .06 log shock. MNI's
median/sigma are1.02/.20, Earrings .98/.15, Babydoll1/.25, Mr.Brightside1/.15,
Stateside .9/.25 and DWS1/.30. Other cohort tracks use .9/.35. Five-million
cumulative/measurement noise per song is also subjective.

Older-song missing weeks use a shared average anchored by visible early-year
and late2025 US observations. APT and Billie Jean are known pre2026 recordings
even if no old US chart cells are visible. Later-starting histories use an
explicit low/pre-release central listening scenario, not factual zero filling.
A10% common exceptional-history branch selects at most one such track for a
larger older/alternate-history path. This avoids independently inventing peak
listening for dozens of unreleased months. The first exploratory version made
that error and was rejected; `spotify_joint_audit.md` records why before any
completed valuation used it. Missing/censored/history assumptions remain weak.

An explicit15% Stateside-method branch adds a hypothetical0/40/110m triangular
amount of *nonduplicated* coverage. It does not claim those extra streams exist.
Ten percent total mass is reserved for unseen/methodology/publication outcomes;
another5% exceptional named-winner mass covers alias or viral surprises. Its
song weights are disclosed in the code, not measured frequencies. These choices
dominate tiny tail probabilities and must not be advertised as calibrated odds.

The raw mixture gives MNI36.92%, Stateside3.68%, Earrings43.77%, and small named
tails. Primary reports **MNI37%, Stateside4%, DWS/Risk/APT/Dai/Swim/Billie/Beauty
0.5% each, Drop Dead/hate1% each**, leaving53.5% for unlisted outcomes. Tail
rounding deliberately avoids treating Monte Carlo zeros as impossibility;
these are uncertainty judgments, not recovered ranks. Broad case ranges allow
material alternate aggregation/history/cutoff results. The four saved parameter
sensitivities are scenarios, not statistical confidence intervals or floors.
