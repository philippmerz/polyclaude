# Primary Spotify model audit

October17. The first expanded54-track exploratory model was **rejected before
any completed evaluation or money**. It independently imputed substantial
listening into long pre-table histories for every late-year release, causing
recent songs to acquire hundreds of millions of invented earlier listens and
even push the observed424m leader into runner-up. Unknown is not zero, but it
is also not a license to multiply a new single's peak week across its unreleased
months. The implausible output was a model defect, not a discovered annual rank.
The rejected script/results are retained in logs/deep_review_20261017 with
rejected_exploratory prefixes; no report adopts them.

The replacement keeps old-song missing-history imputation distinct, uses an
explicit low/pre-release central scenario for later-starting histories, and
a shared rare older-alias branch instead of54 independent high-history events.
This remains an assumption, never a claim absent dates were factually zero.
Release chronology confirms that catalog and first chart dates can differ;
track identity, annual cutoff and aggregation remain open. Source-based
parameter sensitivities and broad forecast ranges must remain visible.
