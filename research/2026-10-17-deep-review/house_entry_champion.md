# House NO-maker champion critique — 2026-10-17

**Independent case for at most the NC-11 small maker; not an order or authoritative forecast.** At the report’s $0.15 NC NO price, a 20-share post-only fill costs $3.00 because the authoritative 4% curve is taker-only; NO win pays $20. The 10-point stress (NO 20%) gives $4 expected payout and **$1.00 stressed profit before carry**. The source case is imperfect but not empty: the named September PPP release had Democrat Jamie Ager ahead 47–44; Q3 FEC cash favored Ager. The model applies a sizable effective poll error and adds national movement/PVI context. Price is materially below the 15% stressed break-even, so if its central view is directionally right, the maker offers attractive payoff versus its bounded $3 principal.

The hardest objection is that 10 points is a policy haircut, not calibrated protection. The corrected sensitivity range reaches 95% Democratic win (5% NO); the one poll is old, incompletely documented, and the 1.5-point cash adjustment is subjective. NC’s stress cushion exists only if central 30% NO remains a reasonable anchor; at the adverse grid edge, the full stake loses. A fresh NC poll, current-map/candidate confirmation, and fill monitoring materially matter.

OH-07 has weaker economics at $0.17 NO: a 20-share maker costs $3.40; its 18% stressed NO probability gives $3.60 expected payout, only **$0.20 before carry**. The cushion is two probability points and disappears with a small price/model move or adverse fill. Its source has a stronger sample description (Tavern’s 635 online likely voters) but is old: candidate preference 54–46 coexisted with a 50–50 generic ballot; the model appropriately discounts the apparent lead. Its corrected 5–95% NO range still admits a full loss.

Hold-cash earns about 1.4¢ on $3 and 1.6¢ on $3.40 over 55 days at the dated 3.0553% Aave sensitivity, but preserves flexibility and avoids model risk. NC alone is the cleaner optionality; adding OH takes committed risk from $3 to $6.40 and adds only $0.20 of OH stress surplus before carry. Both NOs lose together on a Democratic wave: both YES outcomes burn the full **$6.40 principal** (about 3.3% of stated $196 NAV). Their modeled central edges are not independent diversification. Any entry still needs fresh books, source/candidate checks, configured catalyst coverage, and all portfolio/account gates.
